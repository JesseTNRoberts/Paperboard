Urban air quality is usually measured by a handful of regulatory monitors per city [@epa2023]. These stations are accurate but sparse, and short-lived pollution spikes near roads and schools fall between them [@apte2017].

Low-cost sensors promise far denser coverage, but their raw readings drift with humidity and temperature [@karagulian2019].
